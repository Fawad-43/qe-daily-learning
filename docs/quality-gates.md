# QE Daily Quality Gates

Every post must pass these checks before merge/publish:

1. Technical claims are correct and internally consistent.
2. Company architecture, incidents, requirements, customer data, or internal metrics are never invented or exposed.
3. Hypothetical scenarios are clearly framed as hypothetical.
4. The expected investigation does not assume a single root cause without evidence.
5. The challenge develops reasoning rather than recall only.
6. The post distinguishes observable facts from assumptions where relevant.
7. The topic/title is not a duplicate of recent content.
8. Slack copy is concise enough for a 3–5 minute read.
9. LinkedIn copy is suitable for public sharing and contains no internal-only context.
10. The takeaway is accurate and not misleadingly absolute.
